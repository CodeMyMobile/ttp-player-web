Player Code of Conduct

We want every class to be fun, competitive, and safe for everyone on court. By booking a class, you agree to the following.

Zero tolerance (immediate removal)

Throwing, slamming, or hitting a racket in anger; intentionally hitting balls at or near another person; verbal abuse, threats, or harassment of players or coaches. Any of these will result in removal from class and may result in removal from future programs. Refunds for remaining sessions are at the coach's discretion.

Expected on court

Respect your coach, fellow players, and the facility. Keep language clean and emotions in check. Follow the coach's instructions on drills, rotations, and safety. For line calls, scoring, and on-court etiquette, we follow the USTA's Friend at Court.

Coach's discretion

Coaches have final say on class placement and on whether behavior is acceptable. We may end a player's participation at any time if their conduct could be harmful to themselves or others, or affects the safety or enjoyment of the group, even if it isn't listed above.
